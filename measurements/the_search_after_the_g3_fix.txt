The range the encoding always offered is being sampled for the first time. Note
what this is and is not: it says the SEARCH can now reach fast and slow neurons.
It says nothing whatever about whether they help. That question is a claim, it
needs pre-registration and a gate, and it could not even be asked before today.

⚠ THE INSTRUMENT LIED ON ITS FIRST RUN AGAINST THE NEW LINEAGE, and the lie was
predicted in its own comment. `what_is_in_the_whole_roster.escript` carried
`-define(SEED_DRAW, 8192)` under a warning that hardcoding the bound "would make
this lie the day seeding changes". Seeding changed the same day. The first run
reported 1581 of 2184 time constants "outside the band seeding can draw" against
a seeding that now draws the whole range and has no band. It asks `breed` for the
draw widths now, which is what the comment had claimed all along.

A warning written next to a literal is still a literal.

⚠⚠ THE POPULATION NUMBERS ARE NOT YET EVIDENCE FOR THE SEATING CHANGE. Twenty
minutes in, 91 and 71 are what a roster filling from empty looks like, and the
old code would also have been climbing at this stage. The seating fix predicts
they reach 240 and stay there, where the old ones stalled between 60 and 153 over
twelve thousand rounds. That is a measurement for tomorrow, not today, and it
would be dishonest to claim it now.
