# Claims

**Empty, and correctly so.**

`CHARTER.md` classifies this whole track as a **BUILD**. Nothing in the order of
work tests a hypothesis about the world, so nothing in it belongs here. A
directory of pre-registrations for a simulator, a protocol and an exhibit would
be the category error `CLAUDE.md` names: gating a build costs days and produces
documents nobody reads.

## What would go here

A claim about the world, with its pre-registration written **before** the runner
exists.

One directory per claim, named for what it asserts, holding the
pre-registration, the runner, the raw feed and the result together. A claim is a
vertical slice like any other, and the predecessor's flat piles of twenty-three
pre-registrations beside twenty-three results are what that rule exists to
prevent.

## What a claim here would have to respect

⚠ **Programme P7 in `faber-ecosystem` closed on a negative at insight 062**, and
its arc-level result is *every configuration tested was either too decoupled to
escalate or too coupled to survive, with no lever between the two*. Insight 057
refuted reciprocal coupling twice, reproduced at n=80 and again on a
re-implemented engine.

So **"the islands arms-raced" is not a claim this repository may make casually**,
and the design deliberately does not depend on it: raids move opponents rather
than assigning fitness, which is the charter's one idea.

If a claim is ever wanted, the four measurement rules signed at 053 to 056 are
the durable output of that programme and they apply:

1. **Co-fitness is blind to progress.** Two sides improving against each other
   produce a flat score with no trend. Use a fixed benchmark.
2. **A fixed benchmark must be graded or it saturates and goes silently blind.**
   A weak reference set missed roughly 31 of about 35 units of progress.
3. **A misaligned selection proxy decouples from true quality under asymmetric
   costs**, pumping the cheap dimension and abandoning the bottleneck.
4. **Under cycling a fixed benchmark is honest but blind.** Only a master
   tournament sees intransitive structure.

The frozen benchmark in
[../design/DESIGN_THE_ROSTER_AND_THE_RAID.md](../design/DESIGN_THE_ROSTER_AND_THE_RAID.md)
exists because of rules 1 and 2, and it is built before any breeding rather than
alongside a claim, because it cannot be added to history afterwards.

## The likelier first claim, if there is one

Not escalation. **Evolved signalling under interception** is a cooperation
question rather than an arms-race question, with different literature and
different failure modes, and P7 says nothing about it. The instrument for it,
the ablation delta, is built with the channel rather than after it, which is what
would make such a claim askable at all.
